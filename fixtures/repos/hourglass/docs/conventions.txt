Notes on how the modules fit together

models has Span and Shift and the day names. Nothing in there knows about text.

parsing is the only place that deals with how people write times. Everything
past that point is minutes since midnight.

merging is pure arithmetic on spans. No workers, no days, no files. A span from
one day and a span from another mean nothing to it, callers sort that out.

schedule is where shifts, days and workers meet. It can use parsing and merging,
but neither of those should ever reach back into schedule.

Spans never cross midnight. An overnight shift is two shifts on two days, and
whoever reads the schedule file has to split it before it gets here.

End of a span is exclusive. Touching spans do not overlap but they do merge.

Days are the short lowercase names once they are past parsing. Week order comes
from the DAYS tuple and not from any sort on the names.
