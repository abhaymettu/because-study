Notes on how the modules fit together

models holds the plain data holders and nothing else. No file access in there.

reader knows about roster CSV columns. Nothing outside reader should care what
a roster column is called.

attendance owns the status vocabulary. If a new status alias shows up it goes in
the alias table there and the summaries pick it up on their own.

Dates are real date objects by the time they leave attendance. Raw date text
stays inside that module.

summary works on Student and Mark objects it is handed and never opens a file.
It can lean on attendance and models but has no business talking to reader.

Sections are opaque labels. Nothing should assume they are letters or sort in a
meaningful way beyond plain alphabetical.

Files get read once, whole. No streaming, rosters are small.
